# Choose your testing question

Eight public guides for understanding a testing concern and preparing a focused professional discussion. Each provides original orientation, linked official references, a locally runnable mini tool and an optional assistant skill. They do not execute tests or assess your systems.

| Guide | What you can clarify |
| --- | --- |
| [Digital Platform Testing Guide](https://github.com/mr-jonam/digital-platform-testing-guide) | Which assurance question should be clarified before choosing a test approach or asking for a proposal? |
| [SAP Testing Readiness](https://github.com/mr-jonam/sap-testing-readiness) | What business evidence should be discussed for the affected SAP journey? |
| [ServiceNow Testing Readiness](https://github.com/mr-jonam/servicenow-testing-readiness) | What evidence would explain the effect of a workflow change to its owner? |
| [Salesforce Release Confidence](https://github.com/mr-jonam/salesforce-release-confidence) | Which CRM outcome still needs evidence beyond code-level checks? |
| [Dynamics 365 Testing Navigator](https://github.com/mr-jonam/dynamics-365-testing-navigator) | Which application family and business journey determine the evidence discussion? |
| [AI Testing Evidence Guide](https://github.com/mr-jonam/ai-testing-evidence-guide) | What evidence and decision ownership are needed to discuss acceptable AI behaviour? |
| [Platform Performance Brief](https://github.com/mr-jonam/platform-performance-brief) | What demand assumption and user outcome need evidence before discussing a performance engagement? |
| [Web Portal Quality Guide](https://github.com/mr-jonam/web-portal-quality-guide) | Which part of the user journey has an unresolved quality or accessibility question? |

## Turn a useful question into the right support

Read the guide closest to your decision, note one unresolved evidence question, then [discuss it on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/manoj-rollo/). You can contact directly; a completed tool is not required. A professional engagement can be scoped around assessment, testing delivery or ongoing quality support after the actual context and responsibilities are understood. No guaranteed outcome is asserted. Keep the initial message free of client names, system URLs, credentials and internal documents.

> I found [guide]. We are preparing [broad change] on [platform]. Before [decision/timing], we need to clarify [evidence question]. Could we discuss the appropriate support?
