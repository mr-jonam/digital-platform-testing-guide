# Digital Platform Testing Guide: decision guide

Find the testing conversation your next platform change needs.

**For:** Product owners, technology leaders and QA decision makers who need to frame a testing conversation.

**Decision in focus:** Which assurance question should be clarified before choosing a test approach or asking for a proposal?

## Begin with the business decision

A change can be technically deployable while a business outcome remains uncertain. Name the decision first: accept a release, plan an upgrade, investigate slow journeys or evaluate an AI feature. This makes the evidence request more useful than a generic request for more testing.

**Question to discuss:** What outcome would make the change acceptable to the business owner?

## Describe the boundary of the journey

A journey can cross a portal, a packaged platform, an identity provider and an external service. A successful check within one product does not resolve every handover. Identify the systems involved at a category level; do not publish landscape diagrams or endpoint details.

**Question to discuss:** Which handover has no clear evidence owner?

## Ask what each evidence layer tells you

Code tests, interface checks, user acceptance, accessibility evaluation and performance work answer different questions. Their relevance depends on the change. Avoid treating the presence of one tool or a coverage number as a complete explanation of confidence.

**Question to discuss:** Which business question remains unanswered by the evidence already available?

## Keep the release decision accountable

A useful conversation identifies who accepts the business outcome, who can interpret technical evidence and which uncertainty needs an explicit decision. This guide is an editorial orientation aid, not a maturity benchmark or a release approval.

**Question to discuss:** Who can accept or defer each unresolved uncertainty?
